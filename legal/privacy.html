Privacy statement

Revision 5

We collect only what we need to run keyfab and sell you files. We don't track you, advertise to you or sell your data.

Who is responsible

Sixty North AS, Skjellestadhagen 42, 1389 Heggedal, Norway (org. no. 921 587 538) is the controller of personal data processed through keyfab.io. Contact: keyfab@sixty-north.com.

This holding page

This page sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers record visitors' IP addresses for security and operation. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

What we process when keyfab is running

  • Account data: your email address and sign-in details. Sign-in is provided by WorkOS.
  • Your designs: the designs you save and any images or fonts you upload, so that we can store them and generate your files.
  • Orders: what you bought, when, the price, the tax charged and your country. Payment is handled by Stripe, which gives us your name, email address and billing country, but not your full card details.
  • Fault reports: if you report a failed file generation, we file it in our GitHub issue tracker. The report holds a description of the failure and a reference number, not your design or your contact details.
  • Essential cookies and browser storage only: a sign-in session, security protection, and a local copy of your unsaved design. Since there is no tracking and no advertising, there is no need for a cookie banner to request your consent.

Why we process it

We process your data to provide the service and fulfil your purchases (contract), to keep the accounting and tax records the law requires (legal obligation), and to keep the service secure (legitimate interests). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is processed

keyfab is hosted by Clever Cloud in France (EU). WorkOS (sign-in), Stripe (payments) and GitHub (hosting this holding page, and fault reports) may process data in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep account data and designs for as long as you have an account. To delete your account, email keyfab@sixty-north.com from the address you sign in with. We will delete your account and designs within one month. Order and tax records are kept for as long as the law requires (up to 10 years for EU VAT records), even after your account is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time for access to your data or a copy of it, to correct or delete it, or to object to or restrict how we use it. Email keyfab@sixty-north.com, and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, or to the data protection authority where you live.
