What we sell

keyfab is a browser-based designer for custom keyboards, and designing is free. You can buy automatically generated fabrication files for a design, delivered by download: for example circuit-board manufacturing files (Gerbers and drill files), keycap printing specifications and keyboard firmware. Each purchase covers one kind of output for one saved version of one design. We sell no physical goods. If you have a keyboard made from the files, your contract for that is with the manufacturer you choose, not with us.
