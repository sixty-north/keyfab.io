keyfab

Design your own keyboard. Get everything you need to make it real.

keyfab is a browser-based designer for custom mechanical keyboards. Lay out your keys, shape your keycaps, design your legends — then buy the automatically generated digital fabrication files: circuit-board manufacturing files, keycap printing specifications and keyboard firmware, delivered by download, ready for the manufacturers of your choice. No physical goods are sold; your design becomes files, and the files become your keyboard.

Launching October 2026.
